Format SQL Query Online

A 200-line SQL query without formatting is about as readable as a brick wall. You can technically figure out what's going on, but you're not going to enjoy the process. Especially at 4pm on a Friday when production is down and the query was written by someone who left the company six months ago.

That's where formatting SQL queries online becomes a lifesaver. Paste that monster into the input, hit format, and suddenly you can see the structure. The JOIN conditions line up, the subqueries get indented properly, and that seven-level nested CASE statement actually starts making sense.

One thing I've noticed: the formatter handles CTEs particularly well. A lot of tools choke on WITH clauses and turn them into an unreadable mess. This one preserves the logical flow — which matters when you're debugging a chain of recursive CTEs.
