SQL Formatter — No Signup Required

You know that sinking feeling when you Google 'format sql online', click the first result, paste your query, and then — please create an account to see your formatted output. Five seconds of rage, close tab, try the next one.

This tool skips all of that. There is no signup form, no email verification, no 'unlock 10 more formats per month' nonsense. The SQL formatter runs in your browser using JavaScript. The browser does not need to know who you are to indent a SELECT statement.

I think requiring signup for a formatting tool is like requiring a library card to use a pencil sharpener. The sharpener doesn't care who you are, and neither should the formatter. So here we are: paste, format, copy, leave. No accounts, no tracking, no emails.
