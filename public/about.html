About SQLFormat.io

SQLFormat.io is a free, fast, and privacy-focused online SQL formatter and beautifier. All processing happens directly in your browser — we never send your SQL queries to any server.

✨ Why SQLFormat.io?

  • 100% Client-Side — Your data never leaves your device
  • 15+ SQL Dialects — MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite, Snowflake, BigQuery, and more
  • Fast & Lightweight — No sign-ups, no ads, no distractions
  • Free Forever — No hidden limits or premium tiers

🔧 Supported Dialects

Standard SQL, MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite, BigQuery, DB2, Hive, MariaDB, PL/SQL, Redshift, SingleStoreDB, Snowflake, Spark, T-SQL, Trino
