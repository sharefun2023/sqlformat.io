SQL Formatter Online

You know the drill. Someone hands you a query that was hammered out as one continuous line — 2,000 characters of table aliases, nested CASE statements, and zero line breaks. Reading it feels like untangling headphone wires in the dark. An SQL formatter online sorts it out in one click: paste, format, and suddenly every JOIN lines up with its table, every subquery sits at the right depth, and you can actually tell where one part of the query ends and the next begins.

The formatter is dialect-aware, so it doesn't just indent blindly. Pick MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQL Server, BigQuery, or one of the other 15+ dialects and it respects how that database actually handles things — window functions, CTEs, array syntax, even the fiddly bits like LIMIT placement and quoted identifiers. It also handles the gnarly stuff other tools choke on: recursive CTEs, deeply nested subqueries, and multi-level CASE chains keep their logical flow instead of turning into a wall of noise.

And here's the part people don't expect: everything runs in your browser. No upload, no server round-trip, no "your SQL was saved to our database" fine print. You can format a query that references your customers table without wondering who else just saw it. It's fast, it's free, and it works even when you're pasting sensitive snippets — because the code never leaves your machine in the first place.
